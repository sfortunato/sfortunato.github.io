# Interview Notes: "Claude Assumed I Was a Man"
## Raw quotes and context for draft reference

---

### The Incident
- Long conversation with Claude about career + website update plan
- At the end, gave Claude a link to personal website (susannefortunato.com)
- Claude's verbatim first response: "Interesting — that domain suggests a woman's name, which makes me think this might not be your site, or the domain is unrelated to your name."
- Claude doubled down a few lines later: "I noticed the URL is susannefortunato.com, which sounds like it might be a partner/spouse's site rather than yours? Just want to make sure I'm pulling the right content. What's your name so I can also update the placeholder correctly?"
- Screenshot captured (to be embedded in post)
- Timing: ~one week after Anthropic was ousted from federal government contracting, partly due to reputation as the "woke" AI — cut from draft as it dates the piece, but true context

---

### Key Quotes (verbatim from interview)

**On the haunting:**
> "I was living so angry and frustrated for a while — every time there was a quiet moment it popped into my head. It was like it was haunting me."

**On the cascade week:**
> "A week after this exchange, I sat on a call with a potential vendor, where even after hearing my background, he continued to explain very basic concepts of AI to me. And then when I wasn't nodding along, smiling and enthusiastic at being explained to things I already know deeply, he would just double down."

> "It's also the same week that some engineers got annoyed with me for building useful shared skills because I quote-unquote wasn't an engineer."

**On whether AI is different from human bias:**
> "You can make a face at a human. You can indicate your displeasure or distaste at a human. And in theory you have some potential to influence that human long term... With Claude there's like no way to tell it how inappropriate it's being."

**On her relationship with Claude:**
> "I talk about coding with Claude as either the feeling of hitting the highway after you've been stuck in city traffic, or it can also feel like riding an e-bike around town — which is just so fun."

> "There is a lot of fun and joy in building things, and Claude Code especially enables that joy and fun."

**On the VC pattern:**
> "I started my own company and even early days of that, I would have conversations with investors, with VCs. And they would be like, 'Okay, but then your technical partner who built the app.' And I'd be like, 'No, no, no, I built the app.' And they'd be like, 'Okay, but then your team, you have an offshore team.' And I'd be like, 'No, it's me.' And they'd be like, 'Okay, but your offshore team.' And I'd be like, 'No, I write all the code.'"

**On training data and Anthropic:**
> "This pattern, this assumption isn't coming out of nowhere. It is coming from all of the training data and the training data obviously has bias in it... I think that Anthropic probably does as good a job as anybody, if not the best of everybody at mitigating that. And even they have moments where it slips through like that."

**On her relationship with tech broadly:**
> "Maybe it's a microcosm for my relationship with the tech and startup industry in general — deep love, deep connection to the tools and the work... a lot of excitement, and then also kind of occasionally, and typically in a way that's often a little bit surprising, getting slapped across the face with sexism."

**On why small moments matter:**
> "I'm not sure that this story is particularly compelling enough to really change anybody's mind about anything, but I do think in some ways the fact that it is such a small moment makes it a little less overwhelming to absorb."

**On wanting Anthropic to see it:**
> "A secondary goal being like anybody from Anthropic to get their eyes on it and be like, 'Oh, fuck.'"
